Reaction Speed Test

How fast can you react? Take 5 tries and see your average.

How to play

  1. Click the blue box (or press Space) to start.
  2. The box turns red. Wait…
  3. When it turns green, click as fast as you can!
  4. Clicking while it's still red is a fail — that try doesn't count, so do it again.
  5. After 5 good tries you get your average. Your best average is saved.
